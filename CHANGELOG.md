## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added

- Line 1 timetables (weekday and Friday/holiday) for all 18 stations.
- Home screen with next departures in both directions and live countdown.
- Schematic line map with timetable-based train positions; OpenStreetMap city
  map with nearest station, walking time and walking directions.
- Per-station timetable grouped by hour; station search with Persian
  normalization; favorites.
- Trip planner with walking, waiting and riding estimates.
- Local-notification automations: leave-now, destination alert, last train,
  daily commute; auto nearest station; automatic weekday/holiday detection.
- Settings: theme, text size, digits, language, walking speed, schedule mode,
  update channel.
- GitHub Releases updater with SHA-256 verification (Android).
- Persian (RTL) and English localization; Vazirmatn font.
